# HIM Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

HIM (Hotline Instant Messenger) is an instant messenger for the Hotline network, made by
John Leighow. This page covers the HIM apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows and Linux.

## The short version

HIM has no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and its developer doesn't receive your data
through the app. What you send goes to the Hotline server you sign on to, and what HIM
remembers stays on your device.

## What goes to the server you choose

To work, HIM sends these to the Hotline server you sign on to (VesperNet,
`hotline.vespernet.net`, unless you pick another):

- **Your account:** your screen name and password. On secure servers the password is
  checked without being sent in the clear (HOPE), and the connection is encrypted.
- **What you send:** messages, away messages, your display name and profile, your Buddy Icon,
  pictures and files you send, and your Buddy List and buddy requests.
- **Your online status** (available, away, and so on) for your buddies to see.

That server keeps what it needs to deliver them: for example, messages for buddies who are
offline until they sign on, and pictures sent in chat for a limited time. How long each server
keeps things is up to whoever runs it.

**VesperNet**, the default server, is run by HIM's developer. VesperNet keeps your account,
your Buddy List and profile, and messages waiting for someone who's offline. It uses them only
to run the messenger. It doesn't sell them, share them, or use them for advertising. To delete
your VesperNet account, contact us (below).

**Chat rooms** are public chats on Hotline servers run by other people around the world. When
you join one, that server sees the name you use there, your messages and your connection, and
everyone in the room sees what you say. Their own policies apply.

## What stays on your device

- Your conversations (kept so you can scroll back), settings and away messages.
- Your password, if you ask HIM to remember it, in the system's secure store (the Keychain on
  Apple devices).
- Files and pictures buddies send you, saved where you can see them (Downloads/HIM on a
  computer, the HIM folder in Files on iPhone and iPad).

You can clear a conversation from a buddy's Info, and removing HIM removes the rest.

## Other services HIM contacts

- **Hotline trackers**, to list chat rooms: they see your connection, as any website would.
- **hlwiki.com**, for the classic Hotline user icons shown in chat rooms.
- **GitHub**, only if you use **Report**, which opens a page there in your browser.

The BadassBuddy icons come with HIM; picking one contacts no one.

## Children

HIM isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

## Changes
