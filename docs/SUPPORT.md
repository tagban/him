# HIM Help

HIM (Hotline Instant Messenger) is an instant messenger for the Hotline network, in the
spirit of late-90s AIM. This page covers the iPhone, iPad and Mac app, and the classic app
for Mac, Windows and Linux.

**Need a hand?** Open an issue at https://github.com/tagban/him/issues and we'll get back
to you. Please say which app and device you're on.

## Getting started

1. **Get a screen name.** On the welcome screen, tap **Create a Screen Name**. It opens
   VesperNet's sign-up page (https://agora.vespernet.net/messenger). VesperNet is the server
   HIM uses unless you choose another.
2. **Sign on.** Back in HIM, tap **I Have a Screen Name** and enter your screen name and
   password. Turn on **Remember password** and **Sign on automatically** if you like.
3. **Using another Hotline server?** Tap **Use a different server** (or **Server**) and enter
   its address.

No account? **Just browse chat rooms** lets you join public Hotline chats as a guest.

## Buddies

- **Add a buddy:** the person-plus button, then their screen name. They get a request and
  appear once they accept.
- **Suggested buddies:** on VesperNet, HIM offers John (who made HIM) and SmarterChild, a
  chatbot for weather, news, trivia and more. Add them, or tap **Not now**.
- **Requests** from other people appear at the top of Buddies: **Accept** or **Decline**.
- **A buddy's Info** (the ⓘ in a conversation): their profile, a private nickname for them,
  **Block**, **Report**, **Remove**, and **Clear Conversation History**.

## Messages

- Tap a buddy to start a conversation. You'll see **Sent**, **Delivered** and **Read** under
  your messages, and "typing…" when they're typing.
- **Offline buddies** get your message when they next sign on.
- **Emoji** go out as plain text, because classic Hotline clients can't show them: 😀 becomes
  `:D`, 👍 `(Y)`, and others their name, like `:unicorn_face:`. HIM shows them as emoji again.
- **Away:** tap your status under your name and choose an away message. Anyone who messages
  you gets it once as an auto-reply.

## Pictures and files

- **To a buddy:** the **+** beside the message box (on a Mac, you can also drag a file in).
  They choose **Accept** or **Decline**; both of you need to be online. Big photos are made
  smaller first.
- **Where they go:** files from buddies are saved in **Downloads/HIM** on a computer, and in
  the **HIM** folder of the **Files** app on iPhone and iPad.
- **In chat rooms:** the picture button beside the message box, on servers that take pictures.

## Your Buddy Icon and name

Tap your picture at the top of Buddies (classic app: **My HIM > Buddy Icon**):

- **BadassBuddy Icons…** opens the BadassBuddy.com collection that comes with HIM. Icons
  courtesy of [BadassBuddy.com](https://www.badassbuddy.com) ♥
- Or choose your own picture; HIM makes it the right size, and keeps animated GIFs animated.
- **Display name** is the name buddies see; leave it empty to go by your screen name.

## Chat rooms

**Rooms** lists the public chats of Hotline servers around the world. Join one with a tap,
or enter a server's address.

- You see what was said before you came in, on servers that keep chat history.
- **People** shows who's there. Press and hold a name (right-click on a Mac) to **Ignore**
  them (their lines stop showing) or **Report** them.

## Safety

- **Block** a buddy from their Info: they can't message you.
- **Ignore** someone in a chat room from the People list.
- **Report** abuse from a buddy's Info or a room's People list, or at
  https://github.com/tagban/him/issues. We read every report.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Incorrect login"**: check your screen name and password. Forgot it? Use VesperNet's site.
- **Messages only arrive while HIM is open on iPhone.** iOS ends background connections; when
  you open HIM again, messages people sent meanwhile arrive.
- **Can't connect?** Check your internet connection, then try again; HIM reconnects on its own
  when the connection drops.
- **A file didn't go:** both of you must be online and buddies. Try again once you are.
